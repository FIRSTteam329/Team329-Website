The Costs of Robotics

Running a robotics team is not cheap. Each year we rely on our sponsors and donators to fund everything our team does.

There are expenses around every turn from competition entry fees to building supplies to t-shirts to transportation and keeping the team up and running means ensuring we have the capital to do so.

Become a 329 Sponsor

Becoming a Team 329 sponsor is a great way to help support incredible students in STEM. Your sponsorship allows us to continue to compete year after year and give our students amazing opportunities such as real world engineering experience, chances to network with industry professionals, and scholarship opportunities.

Plus, as a registered 501c3 organization, your donation to Team 329 is tax deductible. Additionally, there are perks that come with your donation.
