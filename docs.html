Documentation

Here you will find all of the most important forms and documents associated with robotics. These include necessary documents such as the FRC consent and release form which can be found on your F.I.R.S.T. dashboard as well as the current season materials.

Important Forms

Consent and Release Form:

This form is necessary for any and all participation is F.I.R.S.T. events. All students must fill out (if they are 18) or have a parent fill out (if they are under 18) a consent and release form in order to go to competitions. To access this form, each member must have a F.I.R.S.T. dashboard account and request to be a member of Team 329 if not already a member. The form will appear under “Acknowledgement Forms” after clicking on “Hello, (your name)”.

2026 Rebuilt Game Manual:

The game manual has all of the information about the game we’re getting ready to play. When building and designing our bot we have to strictly abide by the standards set forth in this manual. Additionally, we also have to base our design on what’s going to get us the most ranking points. Study up on the game, game pieces, field, and bot specifications with the manual.
