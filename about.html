Team 329: The Mohawks

We are Patchogue-Medford High School FIRST Robotics Team 329, also known as The Mohawks. Since our founding in 1999, our team has proudly participated in the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC), an international program that challenges students to design, build, and compete with industrial-size robots. Each year, we are presented with a brand-new game and a unique set of challenges that must be solved within a limited timeframe. Students work together in teams to design, build, program, and test a fully functional robot while also managing aspects such as marketing, outreach, and team organization. This experience allows members to develop real-world skills in engineering, teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving. Beyond competition, Team 329 is deeply committed to community outreach and spreading the mission of FIRST. We actively engage with our community through demonstrations, mentorship, and events that promote STEM education and inspire younger students. Through these efforts, we aim to share the core values of FIRST, including Gracious Professionalism© and Coopertition©, which emphasize respect, collaboration, and integrity both on and off the field. Our team is more than just robotics—we are a family that supports one another, celebrates creativity, and works together to make a positive impact in our school and community.
